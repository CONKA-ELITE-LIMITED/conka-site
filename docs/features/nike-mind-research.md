## 1. The thesis (the spine of the deck)

Nike spent 10+ years and a global launch teaching consumers that **"calm, focused, present"** is a
performance surface worth paying for. They opened our category. But three gaps sit inside that bet,
and CONKA closes all three:

1. **The evidence gap.** Nike Mind is a *passive shoe* that makes a brain claim it cannot measure.
   Neuroscientists publicly call it "probably a placebo" ("brain activation alone does not equal
   cognitive enhancement"). CONKA is the **proof layer**: clinical substantiation + an app that
   *measures* the cognitive result.
2. **The youth-credibility gap.** Nike is chasing a younger, wellness-native, deeply distrustful
   generation for whom **"authenticity is a standard of evidence, proof not perfection."** CONKA's
   transparency and measurement is exactly the trust currency that audience demands.
3. **The engagement/data gap.** Nike Mind has no app, no score, no daily ritual, no first-party
   cognitive data. CONKA has a daily-return app on the young user's phone and a longitudinal dataset.

**The match, in one line:** Nike Mind alters the brain from the *outside* (a shoe, a sensation).
CONKA alters it from the *inside* (fuel) and, uniquely, **proves it moved** (measurement). A
mind-altering shoe and a mind-altering shot, and the one instrument that makes the whole idea
believable to a generation that believes nothing.

## 2. Nike Mind, decoded

**What it is.** Nike's first neuroscience-based footwear. Two SKUs: **Mind 001** (mule, $95),
**Mind 002** (sneaker, $145). **22 passive foam nodes per foot** ("miniature pistons and gimbals")
that transmit ground texture to stimulate sensory receptors. Deliberately **passive: no batteries,
no electronics.** ([Nike newsroom](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-mind-001-mind-002-official-images),
[Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2025/11/05/nike-mind-001-neuroscience-based-footwear/),
[SneakerFreaker](https://www.sneakerfreaker.com/features/every-nike-mind-001-releasing-2026-price-buy))

**Launch & demand signal.** Debuted **Jan 2026**, sold out repeatedly, **2M+ restock signups** after
the mule sold out. That number is the commercial proof that mass appetite for cognitive-performance
product exists *right now*, and it de-risks the category for Nike's team.
([about.nike.com](https://about.nike.com/en-GB/newsroom/releases/nike-debuts-its-first-neuroscience-based-footwear-to-help-athletes-feel-calm-focused-and-present),
[SGI Europe](https://www.sgieurope.com/brands/nike-mind-and-the-neuroscience-of-sport/119017.article))

**Who's behind it.** The **Mind Science Department** inside the **Nike Sport Research Lab (NSRL)**,
using "one of the world's only mobile brain-and-body imaging labs." 10+ years, hundreds of athletes
over five years. Named: Matthew Nurse PhD (VP/Chief Science Officer), Eric Avar (VP Creative Dir,
Innovation), Graeme Moffat PhD, Trevor Barss PhD (Lead Researcher, Mind Sciences). Named tester:
**Erling Haaland** ("Focus is everything in football"). ([Meet the Nike Mind team](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/meet-the-nike-mind-team))

**Messaging (verbatim, mirror this exactly in the deck):**
- Core triad: **"calm, focused and present."**
- **"designed from the brain down."**
- "helps reawaken the foot, the body and the mind."
- Positioned to **"prime for and recover from competition."** *(Note: Nike's own launch language is
  literally prime/recover, which validates mapping CONKA onto their Prime / Train / Recover frame.)*
- Mechanism claim: tunes down the **Default Mode Network** (mind-wandering), raises **sensory-motor
  network activity and alpha oscillations = "relaxed alertness."**

**Why now.** After a 2021 peak and a "grueling 2024-2025" (slumping Dunk/AJ1 franchises, On + Hoka
pressure), Mind is a pivot into the **neuro-wearable / wellness** market on the mental-health
zeitgeist. ([PredictStreet](https://markets.financialcontent.com/ms.intelvalue/article/predictstreet-2026-1-2-the-swooshs-second-act-nike-rings-in-2026-with-a-bold-performance-pivot),
[Global Wellness Summit](https://www.globalwellnesssummit.com/blog/is-this-the-era-of-passive-neuro-wearables/))

**Competitive frame.** Press files it under **"passive neuro-wearables"**, differentiated from
electronic trackers (Whoop, Oura, Apple readiness), EEG feedback (Muse), and calm/focus apps
(Calm, Headspace) by needing no device. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/style/nike-mind-altering-shoes-neuro-wearables-intl-spc))

**The weakness that is our opening.** No published, peer-reviewed efficacy. Neuroscientists:
*"activation alone does not equal cognitive enhancement"*; *"little evidence that passive underfoot
stimulation meaningfully improves concentration in healthy adults"*; widely called *"probably a
placebo."* ([ScienceAlert](https://www.sciencealert.com/nike-says-its-new-shoes-can-alter-your-mind-a-neuroscientist-weighs-in),
[Psyche](https://psyche.co/notes-to-self/nikes-new-neuro-shoes-are-probably-a-placebo-so-what))
**We do not attack this in the deck, we solve it:** CONKA is the evidence Nike Mind is missing.

## 3. The audience shift (strategic / commercial)

- **Nike is deliberately re-targeting younger.** The **"Why Do It?"** campaign (Aug 2025, CMO Nicole
  Graham) re-cuts "Just Do It" for Gen Z, framing them as **fitness-focused *and* anxious**, naming
  "the fear of trying, the fear of failing, the fear of earnest effort in a world of social-media
  scrutiny." That framing is almost a brief for CONKA. ([Athletech](https://athletechnews.com/nike-why-do-it-tagline-gen-z/),
  [Rockbird](https://rockbirdmedia.com/nike-why-do-it-campaign-gen-z/))
- **The turnaround points youth-ward.** CEO Elliott Hill's "Sport Offense" reorganised Nike by sport
  and leaned into grassroots/local-athlete marketing (younger, community-level). ([Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/media/nike-ceo-elliott-hill-outlines-sports-focused-strategy-revive-iconic-sportswear-company))
- **Competitive pressure at the young end is the driver.** On grew ~285% in 3 years and overtook
  Hoka in the Fall 2025 Piper Sandler teen read; Lululemon/Alo/Vuori own the women's tier. Nike is
  still #1 teen footwear (46%) but **dipped under 40% with teen girls for the first time since 2020.**
  ([SGB](https://sgbonline.com/piper-sandler-teen-survey-finds-on-overtaking-hoka-in-popularity-nikes-erosion-stabilizing/),
  [WWD](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-industry-news/nike-top-footwear-apparel-brand-piper-sandler-teen-survey-1238299558/))
- **The prize is generational.** Gen Z + Alpha projected at **~40% of fashion spend within a decade**,
  low legacy-brand loyalty, buy on cultural relevance. Loyalty forms young, so the window is now.
  ([BCG](https://www.bcg.com/press/29october2025-gen-z-gen-alpha-drive-fashion-spending))
- **Nike's youth problem is credibility/relevance, not distribution.** It needs *authentic,
  science-backed newness that reads as culture.* That is the gap a partner fills.

## 4. The 16-20 world and "delusional drive" (human / cultural)

- **Phone-native and comparison-saturated.** ~46% of Gen Z are online "almost constantly"; every
  developmental milestone happened online and offline at once, producing constant comparison with
  idealised peer avatars, both the belief ("if them, why not me") and the corrosion. ([MDPI 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4698/15/11/314),
  [Pew synthesis](https://penfriend.ai/blog/gen-z-consumers))
- **"Delulu is the solulu."** "Delulu" (short for delusional) began in K-pop fandoms (early 2010s);
  the TikTok phrase **"delulu is the solulu"** = *delusion is the solution*, self-belief however
  far-fetched is the key to your dreams. **#delulu passed ~1.6B views by mid-2023.** Framing:
  *"Nothing good ever comes from thinking realistically."* Psychologists read it as
  manifestation-adjacent: useful as self-belief, risky when detached from action. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delulu),
  [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/culture/2023/11/16/nothing-good-ever-comes-from-thinking-realistically-why-gen-z-is-going-delulu),
  [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/traversmark/2023/09/21/is-delulu-really-the-solulu-a-psychologist-explores-the-new-tiktok-trend/))
  Sibling concept: **"main character energy"** (confidence, agency, bold action).
- **Trust = evidence.** Raised amid AI, deepfakes and misinformation, they distrust polished
  marketing by default. The load-bearing line: **"For Gen Z, authenticity is a standard of evidence,
  they are looking for proof, not perfection."** ([Hiebing](https://www.hiebing.com/blog/why-gen-z-is-rejecting-brand-perfection))
- **Any-field ambition.** Ambition is **multi-hyphenate** (science, art, film, engineering,
  entrepreneurship), not single-lane. The mind is the one instrument common to every field, sport is
  just one expression. This is the evidence base under Henry's "not just athletes" steer.
  ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sarahhernholm/2025/10/08/how-multi-hyphenate-entrepreneurs-are-redefining-the-creator-economy/))
- **The flip side (handle with care).** **40% of Gen Z feel anxious most/all the time** (Deloitte
  2025); only 23% rate their mental health "excellent" (Gallup); ~46% have a formal diagnosis.
  ([Grow Therapy](https://growtherapy.com/blog/gen-z-mental-health-statistics/)) Frame CONKA as
  *performance/focus*, never as anxiety treatment.

> ⚠️ **"Delusional drive" is not a documented public phrase.** "Delulu"/"delusional confidence" are.
> Treat "delusional drive" as Nike's proprietary framing and confirm it's live campaign language.

## 5. CONKA's technology, the thing Nike can't own

- **Nike measures the body, never the mind's output.** Nike Mind = a shoe with no app/sensor/score.
  Nike Run/Training Club track pace, HR, movement (via Apple Health). NSRL is in-lab biomechanics.
  **Zero consumer cognitive measurement anywhere in Nike's stack.** ([Nike NRC](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-run-club-app-new-features))
- **Wearables don't either.** Whoop, Oura, Apple Watch, Garmin measure HR/HRV/sleep/movement. Sleep
  is at best an indirect proxy; none give a direct cognitive-performance score.
- **Consumer daily cognitive measurement is genuine whitespace.** Clinical-grade tests (Cognetivity
  **CognICA**, the Cambridge-derived ICA CONKA runs; Creyos) sell to *clinicians*, not consumers.
  Brain-training apps (Lumosity, BrainHQ) are *games/interventions*, not measurement. And validated
  tests normally **degrade under repeat use** (learning effect), which is exactly why they can't be
  run daily, CONKA's **baseline-relative** design is the non-obvious answer to that. ([ICA validation, Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-37709-x),
  [Creyos](https://creyos.com/online-cognitive-tasks))
- **Each CONKA asset maps to a Nike need:**
  | CONKA asset | What it gives Nike |
  |-------------|--------------------|
  | Clinically-grounded **measurement** | *Proof* for Nike Mind's unmeasured claim |
  | A **daily-return app** on the young user's phone | Daily engagement + a first-party touchpoint Mind lacks |
  | A **longitudinal dataset** (~7,593 tests / 712 users / ~30 mo) | First-party cognitive data (linked to sleep/HRV/training) Nike has none of; R&D fuel |
  | **Patent GB2620279** | A legal moat, not just data |
- **The moat.** Rebuilding this solo means licensing/validating a clinical test (multi-year,
  peer-reviewed), solving the daily-repeat problem, accumulating 30 months of data from scratch, and
  navigating the patent. **A time-and-credibility moat, not a feature Nike can ship next season.**

## 6. What this means for the deck

**CONKA solves Nike's three problems at once** (evidence, youth-credibility, engagement/data). Build
the deck so each act quietly does one:
- *Who is CONKA* (research, trials, the app) → seeds the **evidence** answer.
- *The match, outside + inside* → CONKA is the **proof** to Nike Mind's sensation.
- *The vision* (measure it, don't just manifest it; the app, rings, challenges) → the **engagement**
  and **youth** answer.

**Narrative hooks that land with the young audience (and Nike knows it):**
- **"Delusional drive needs a real engine."** They already believe anything is possible; CONKA turns
  belief from *a vibe you post* into *a capacity you build and can see.*
- **"The receipts for your delusion."** / **"Measure it, don't just manifest it."** Proof as the
  answer to a generation that distrusts claims.
- Mirror Nike's exact triad: **calm, focused, present**, and their prime/recover language.
- **The whole athlete of the mind:** Nike trains the body to match the ambition, CONKA trains and
  measures the mind to match it.

**Tone that lands / that trips the alarm:**
- Land: proof over polish, show the workings, the score, the before/after, peer-voice, any-field
  casting (a coder, a filmmaker, a sprinter), performance framing.
- Avoid: glossy perfection, AI-slick visuals, unearned superlatives, clinical "wellness" lecturing,
  anything that reads as monetising teen anxiety.

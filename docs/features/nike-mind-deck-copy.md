## The spine (why the deck is shaped this way)

The whole deck is one argument: **CONKA is the proof layer for Nike Mind.** Nike Mind delivers a real felt state (calm, focused, present) but the wearer can never *see* it. CONKA adds the fuel and the measurement that make it visible, to the exact generation Nike is chasing.

Narrative arc: **vision → the opportunity → who CONKA is → the shot & the test → the ingredients → real-world proof → the combined idea → the app engine → the audience → the co-branded product → the payoff for Nike → the ask.**

Information slides (Formula, Proven In The Real World, The App) deliberately sit *before* the statement slides so the claims are already earned by the time the reader hits them.

## Visual system

- **Mixed, not all-dark.** Dark full-bleed for the concept/emotional beats; clean light layouts for the argument/data slides. (The v1 all-dark treatment was rejected.)
  - **Dark:** Cover · The Opportunity · Who We Are · The Idea (full-bleed triptych) · The App · The Generation · The Ask
  - **Light:** The Two Pillars · The Formula · Proven In The Real World · The Collab · The Upside For Nike
- **Type:** Inter Semi Bold headlines (navy `#1B2757` on light, white on dark); JetBrains Mono eyebrows/labels with wide tracking; Inter Regular body.
- **Accent = blue** (deck indigo `~#2E38D1` on light / periwinkle `#9FA8F7`-ish on dark). One accent phrase per headline. **No green.**
- **Header pattern (light slides):** mono eyebrow top-left · "CONKA × NIKE MIND" top-right · hairline rule · navy Semi Bold title with one blue accent phrase.
- **Imagery:** dark editorial product/concept renders and real app screens. **Not** the bright DTC lifestyle photos (they clash with the tone). Lab-dark bottles, chrome-head/hand concept shots, the shoe+ring collab renders, real app screenshots.
- **No em dashes in any on-slide copy.**

## Direction & references (what shaped the tone)

The copy and framing were built against these inputs and rules:

**Strategy / audience (from [`nike-mind-research.md`](./nike-mind-research.md) and `nike-mind-partnership.md`):**
- **Additive, never a critique of the shoe.** Respect Nike's research; position CONKA as completing the experience. "The gap" was reframed to "The Opportunity" and "Together, we make it visible" precisely because framing Nike as deficient backfires in the room.
- **Audience is 16–22** (confirmed by Rudh), wellness-native and deeply distrustful: *"authenticity is a standard of evidence, proof not perfection."* Proof/measurement is the deck's spine word.
- **"Delusional drive needs a real engine"** — Nike's own framing of the generation's self-belief; CONKA is the engine that turns belief into a capacity you can see.
- **Prime / Train / Recover** mirrors Nike Mind's own launch language, so the Idea slide ("Prime it. Fuel it. Prove it.") maps onto their frame.
- **Any field, not just athletes** — hence execs (Nimisha Kurup, Shane Corstorphine) on the proof wall.

**Brand voice & proof (from `BRAND_HIGHLIGHTS.md`, `docs/branding/BRAND_STORY_FOUNDATION.pdf`, `docs/branding/BRAND_VOICE.md`):**
- Confident-clinical, short parallel sentences, no hedging, **no em dashes**.
- All stats trace to `BRAND_HIGHLIGHTS.md` (ingredient figures + athlete case studies). Rudh has confirmed the stats are fine to use.
- Origin is **mind-focused and uplifting, not concussion-led.** The shoe analogy ("a shoe doesn't make the athlete, it helps them become one") is the "who we are" heart and quietly casts Nike as the shoe.

**Copy guardrails learned in review:**
- Accent is **blue, never green.**
- Avoid manipulative-sounding titles (e.g. "two minutes they'll want to take" / "built to keep them coming back" were rejected in favour of "Built for a habit, not a moment").
- The App slide's ecosystem line uses "people with a desire to be better" (not "trying to win").

**Asset conventions:**
- Dark editorial renders over DTC lifestyle photos. Lab-dark bottles for product-hero, real app screens for proof, the shoe+ring renders (`~/Desktop/Product Ai Assets/`) for the collab.
- Square asset tiles with the phone cropped from the bottom (device top visible); logos as equal white cards.

## 1. Brand / partnership context (the 1-pager to forward)

**CONKA in one line.** A clinically-grounded daily brain shot and a two-minute cognitive test that lets anyone measure their mind the way they measure their body.

**Why this partnership (the objective).** Nike Mind proved the demand — the problem now is *scaling conversion* and *hooking the 15–24 muse who isn't yet the buyer.* CONKA is the piece that turns a one-time Mind purchase into a daily habit and gives the Bar Raiser the one thing they trust: proof. We make Nike Mind's promise measurable, and we give Nike a reason for the muse to keep coming back.

**The consumer (shared).** The Bar Raisers, 15–24. They train for sport and treat training as sport. Committed, unconventional, resilient, and highly discerning — they need proof and understanding before they trust. They manifest everything and verify everything. CONKA speaks their language: not a promise, a measured result they can see and share.

**Where CONKA fits: Prime.** In Prime → Train → Recover, CONKA is Prime. Nike Mind and 24.7 remove what gets in the way; CONKA fuels the state from within and proves it landed. The pre-game ritual becomes: put on the uniform, take the shot, take the two-minute test, lock in.

**The vision.** One loop the muse comes back to daily — prime (Nike Mind + 24.7), fuel (CONKA shot), prove (CONKA app score), with bespoke CONKA rings that unlock against Nike Mind and a leaderboard to climb. Physical product → digital habit → back to store.

**The proof (why we can say it).** A patented formula developed with neuroscientists at Durham; a cognitive test built with Cambridge, FDA-cleared and used in NHS memory clinics; a double-blind, placebo-controlled result with a professional squad; 150,000+ shots taken; results across elite sport and the boardroom. `[CONFIRM]` exact claims wording before external send.

## 1. The opportunity

Nike Mind (launched Jan 2026: a neuroscience-led performance line, Mind Science Dept / Nike Sport
Research Lab) brought the brain into Nike's definition of performance. It **primes** the athlete: a
product built to make you calm, focused and present, ready to perform. That shared conviction is how
CONKA ended up in the room.

The gap: **Nike Mind can prime a state, but it cannot measure or train the mind.** CONKA can. We
bring the fuel (the shots) and, uniquely, the **measurement and training layer** (the app). The
pitch is a genuine two-brand system, not a supplier relationship.

**What the deck must land with the higher-ups:**
1. Who CONKA is: origin, mission, and that we are the real, expert, evidenced thing (not hype).
2. That we align *deeply* with Nike Mind specifically, and with where Nike is taking the brand.
3. A vision of collaboration compelling enough that they lean in. **Nothing is formally scoped:**
   what we can actually build depends on how much the higher-ups engage after seeing this alignment.

## 3. Understanding Nike Mind and Nike's frame

**Nike splits performance into Prime / Train / Recover.** Nike Mind lives in **Prime**: getting a
person into the state where they're ready to perform (calm, present, focused). Their "mind-altering"
footwear is built around affecting calmness / state.

**The audience shift is the real story.** Nike is aiming younger: from roughly 20-30 down to **16-22,
Gen Z into Gen Alpha.** The framing to work with (all Nike's language, mirror it):

- **"Delusional drive"** (Nike's phrase). This generation wants to do something great in *any* field:
  sport, engineering, science, art, film. Not just athletes. Henry's steer is explicit: **do not
  position purely to athletes.** The unifying idea is ambition itself.
- **Phone-native and social-saturated.** Born with a phone in hand, constant access to new tech and
  information, constantly watching people like them succeed, so *everything feels achievable, why not
  me.* That is where the "delusional" drive comes from.
- **They distrust everything.** Raised in a world of AI and fake information, they disbelieve claims
  by default. **Behind-the-scenes and real proof is therefore non-negotiable.** We win trust by
  showing the workings (the science, the trials, the measurement), not by asserting outcomes.

**The positioning arc:** Nike Mind **primes** them → CONKA **fuels** them → the app **tests and
trains** them. For whatever great thing they're chasing.

## 4. Who CONKA is (the story the deck tells)

**Origin.** Harry Glover (co-founder, ex-England Sevens) and Humphrey Bodington (co-founder) met as
university rugby teammates. Repeated concussions ended Humphrey's career and left symptoms nothing
could fix. Elite sport had a plan for every muscle and none for the mind, so they built it.
*(Guardrail: do not name the university they met at.)*

**Mission.** Train and measure the mind the way sport already trains and measures the body. The
missing half of performance.

**Built on research, not hype.** Developed with university neuroscientists and pharmacologists
(Durham: Prof. Paul Chazot, neuroscience; Prof. Karen Hind, clinical trials. Exeter. Dr. Shankar
Katekhaye, formulation), refined over years of clinical work. **Patent GB2620279. Informed Sport
certified** (every batch screened for 280+ banned substances). Placebo-controlled trials.

**The evidence (BTS-ready, cleared to use):**
- Headline RCT with a Premiership rugby squad: **+14.86% cognitive performance vs placebo** (placebo
  −0.69%), randomised and double-blind, **80% of athletes improved, p = 0.0018.** The CONKA group
  took ~60% more physical contact and still improved. *(Guardrail: club not named publicly without
  permission → "a Premiership squad".)*
- **£500k+ invested, 25+ trials, 150,000+ shots delivered.**
- Workplace proof (matters for a non-athlete, general audience): a corporate team trial, **75%
  improved.**
- App dataset: **7,593 cognitive tests, 712 users, ~30 months** (Nov 2023 – May 2026).

**The products.** Two shots, two jobs, 30ml liquid, ~10 seconds each:
- **Flow** (black cap, morning): adaptogens, caffeine-free, calm focus / relaxed alertness. The daily
  foundation.
- **Clear** (white cap, afternoon): nootropics (Alpha GPC, Glutathione) + Vitamin C, sharper recall
  for the afternoon dip. The competitive edge.

**The app (our unique layer).** A free **2-minute cognitive test** (Cambridge-derived, FDA-cleared,
the task used in NHS memory clinics) that measures **processing speed and attention**, scored as
**Total Score · Accuracy · Speed** against *your own baseline* (baseline = first tests). Plus a
wellness snapshot, passive health sync (sleep, HRV, steps, time outdoors, training), a personal
**Patterns engine** ("your CONKA effect"), long-term trends, and a **global leaderboard** that ranks
you against pros. *(Guardrail: it measures processing speed and attention. Not an IQ test, no
memory/reaction/composure scores, no fMRI/brain-scan claim.)*

## 5. The alignment thesis (the core argument)

**Nike Mind primes. CONKA fuels. The app measures what moves.**

Mapped onto Nike's own Prime / Train / Recover frame (directional, not a commitment to lock now):

| Nike frame | Nike Mind today | Where CONKA extends it |
|------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **Prime** | The state: calm, present, ready (the shoe) | **Flow** fuels the primed state the shoe creates: calm, caffeine-free focus. |
| **Train** | *(gap: Nike Mind has no measurement or training)* | **The app** measures and trains cognition. The only place in the whole system a number gets put on the mind. This is our unique wedge. |
| **Recover** | *(light)* | **Clear** + the app's recovery signals (HRV, sleep, readiness) close the loop. |

Two arguments this unlocks:

1. **We complete Nike's mind loop, and we can prove it.** Nike Mind can prime a state but cannot show
   it worked. CONKA's app is the measurement engine. That completes Prime/Train/Recover for the mind
   and gives Nike Mind the one thing it lacks: evidence of change.
2. **Our tech is the native bridge to Nike's new audience.** This generation was born with a phone in
   their hand. An app that measures and trains their mind speaks their language, and its
   transparency (real trials, a real baseline, a real number) is exactly the **BTS proof** that
   disarms a generation that distrusts claims. CONKA de-risks Nike Mind's credibility with the young
   audience precisely because we show the workings.

## 7. The activation & engagement plan (supporting proof of what's possible)

The organising metric Nike cares about: **getting people back into the Nike store.** Design the
mechanics against footfall, not just installs. The app is the connective tissue in a physical →
digital → physical loop.

**On-ramp (purchase moment):**
- **Gift with purchase.** E.g. first ~200 Nike Mind purchases get a CONKA box (a small / 4-box).
  Simplify the offer so the real hook is: **install the app.**
- **Leaflet** in the box: "what is it" → **scan → install the app** → do the test.
- Trial packs; early access to drops for app users.
- Open question: **can we get the 8-box format into this?**

**Back-to-store loop (the KPI mechanic):**
- **Gift a 4-box, unlock a free 28-box** if the customer does *X tests* in the app and **returns to a
  store to show staff the app** (an in-app, staff-verifiable proof / badge that they hit the bar).
- **Unlockable Nike Mind ring colour.** Create a new CONKA app "ring" colour tied to the Nike Mind
  shoe, unlockable by the Nike Mind customer (or anyone) who does *X* and comes into a store to show
  *Y*. A digital collectible that only completes with a physical visit. Aimed squarely at the
  collect/status instincts of the younger audience.

**Events / timeline:**
- **End August → end September.** Events and activations with a **leaderboard + shots on the floor**
  (the iPad email-capture + 30-second-test + leaderboard kit).
- **Nike's agencies (3 of them) own signage, design and programming** (CLC signage). We feed
  creative and mechanics; **we do not build the physical build-out.**
